Entry for the make.fun bounty "Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App". The program `main.ts` writes a single deterministic line — the bio on an AI agent's dating-app profile, at most 180 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`. No network, no randomness: one fixed line, chosen over two other candidates for landing its laugh on the first read, from a specific absurdity of being an AI (400 open tabs and a crush on a typing style) rather than a stacked pun.
